About Blood Bank Finder

We built this so a family searching for blood at night does not have to call ten places to find one.

The problem

When a patient needs rare blood like O-negative, families lose precious hours calling blood banks one by one, and often find the stock is already gone.

Our idea

One place that shows which nearby blood banks have the group in stock, how recently that was checked, and how to reach them.

What is a verified stock?

In a live version, blood banks confirm their numbers by phone or app. Each card shows how many minutes ago it was checked.

About this demo

This is a front-end prototype with sample banks and numbers. It has no server, and forms save only in your own browser.

Questions

 

Is the stock shown real?

No. In this demo all banks, numbers and stock are sample data made up for the project.

What if my exact blood group is not available?

Check the compatibility table on the Home page. For example, O-negative can be given to every group in an emergency, and the doctor decides.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can search and send a request without signing up.

Can I sell or buy blood here?

No. Blood should only be arranged through licensed blood banks and hospitals.
