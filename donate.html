Become a blood donor

One donation can help up to three people. Register once and be ready when someone nearby needs your group.

Can you donate?

The usual rules at most blood banks. The doctor at the centre makes the final decision.

Age

18 to 65 years.

Weight

At least 50 kg.

Haemoglobin

12.5 g/dL or more.

Gap

3 months after the last donation.

Donation day

 

1

Register and check-in

Short form and a quick health check.

2

Donate

The donation itself takes about 10 minutes.

3

Rest and refresh

Have a snack and a drink, and avoid heavy exercise for the day.
